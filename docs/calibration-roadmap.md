# Staged calibration roadmap

Status: proposed; no calibration actions have been performed by this repository contribution. Use the Ender 5 S1 audit as a baseline, not as proof that settings are calibrated. For the other printers, inventory their Marlin settings first and adapt the sequence after identifying hardware.

The [2 November local slicer audit](slicer-audit-2026-11-02.md) now records actual saved profiles, overrides, missing custom S1 mapping and compatibility conflicts. Resolve those stage-1 findings using the [proposed tests and rollback plan](slicer-change-plan.md), then record material-specific results with the [filament template](../shared/templates/filament-calibration.md). No profile correction has been applied.

Every stage needs a dated record, fixed material/nozzle/test conditions, raw private evidence retained outside Git, sanitized results, an acceptance decision and a rollback path. Proceed one controlled change at a time. Obtain explicit owner approval before firmware flashing, heating or movement; separately plan any required restart or configuration save. Never interrupt a print.

| Stage | Purpose | Automatable later, after approval | Physical work / acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Slicer and start/end audit | Inventory existing presets; confirm physical nozzle and align declarations. Keep fresh probing every Ender 5 S1 print. Verify printer-specific purge/park and temperature order. | Compare exported profiles; inspect generated commands, mesh order and hidden overrides; prepare a proposed mismatch warning. | Confirm installed nozzle, hardware, filament and selected presets. No test print yet. |
| 2. Mechanical, thermal and extrusion checks | Inspect belts/wheels/bed play, verify temperature behavior and PID at intended conditions, then measure extruder travel. Do not confuse flow tuning with rotation distance. | Approved temperature logging/PID tests and small extrusion increments; calculate proposed rotation distance from measured travel. | Inspect mechanics, attend heating, mark/measure filament, identify slipping. No model print required. |
| 3. Bed, probe and first layer | Verify repeatability, tram, set Z reference, evaluate twist only if justified, and validate a fresh mesh. | Approved probe routines, screw-adjustment calculations, mesh comparison and first-layer test preparation. | Turn screws, assess nozzle height, print and judge first-layer adhesion/squish across the bed. Recheck offset after geometry changes. |
| 4. Filament temperature and flow | Use known dry material and establish repeatable temperature/extrusion multiplier at conservative speed. | Prepare controlled coupons/towers; analyze user measurements and images. | Print; assess bonding, surfaces, dimensions and top layers. Temperature changes may require thermal/Z rechecks. |
| 5. Input shaping | Measure resonances and validate detail/smoothing. No sensor is configured in the prior S1 audit. | After sensor installation: approved noise/measurement capture and shaper analysis; otherwise prepare ringing prints and calculations. | Confirm/mount sensor or print and measure ringing. Assess quality; no invented resonance frequencies. |
| 6. Pressure advance | Tune after the extrusion/flow and shaping baseline for the chosen nozzle/material. | Prepare a controlled PA test and calculate candidate values; ensure slicer commands do not invalidate the test. | Print and identify best corners/lines; record conditions and accepted value. Zero PA is the observed starting point, not a tuned result. |
| 7. Retraction and seams | Revisit retraction after PA; investigate the prior job's extra restart extrusion. | Prepare controlled tests; compare supplied photos. | Print and assess stringing, seams, blobs and gaps. Do not conceal wet filament with excessive retraction. |
| 8. Acceleration and maximum volumetric flow | Validate motion progressively, characterize sustained flow, then set conservative working caps. | Prepare approved tests; compare requested flow and motion with measured results; inspect per-feature overrides. | Attend motion tests and prints; detect skipped steps, under-extrusion, ringing and cooling limits. Do not treat configured maximums as proven. |
| 9. Regression and release | Validate a representative useful part with balanced quality and speed. | Compare final diffs; record profile/config hashes, results and rollback instructions. | Print, measure fit and inspect finish. Owner accepts results before marking a setup tested. |

References: Klipper's [rotation-distance guide](https://www.klipper3d.org/Rotation_Distance.html), [configuration/PID checks](https://www.klipper3d.org/Config_checks.html), [bed mesh](https://www.klipper3d.org/Bed_Mesh.html), [resonance compensation](https://www.klipper3d.org/Resonance_Compensation.html), [sensor measurements](https://www.klipper3d.org/Measuring_Resonances.html) and [pressure advance](https://www.klipper3d.org/Pressure_Advance.html). Use the documentation matching the installed firmware version when executing a later approved stage.
